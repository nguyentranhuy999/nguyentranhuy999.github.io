Sleep-Inspired Continual Learning

A day–night learning cycle that locally acquires new tasks, audits parameter changes, and consolidates trusted knowledge through replay.

  • Represents task knowledge as gated parameter-delta fragments with learned trust scores.
  • Evaluates catastrophic forgetting on class-incremental MNIST against fine-tuning, replay, and joint-training baselines.
